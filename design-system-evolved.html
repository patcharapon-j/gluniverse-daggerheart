Implementation note

Translate the language into Foundry application shells, actor sheets, item sheets, dialogs, and chat cards.
Foundry contract / practical guardrails
The system is expressive at large scale, disciplined at window scale, and unmistakable at a glance.
WindowsKeep texture fixed; avoid expensive blur inside moving app surfaces.
InteractionMinimum 36px controls, visible focus, full reduced-motion behavior.
DensityAt 700px and below, collapse ornament before collapsing information.
