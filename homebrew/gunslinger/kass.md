# Kass conversion examples

Version 0.1 · Builds for testing the new class

The supplied PF2e export describes Kass as a level-11 male Catfolk, around 40, with the Sailor background and Way of the Drifter Gunslinger class. His selected abilities include sword-and-pistol combinations, boarding and rope work, aid and crew tactics, and several luck features. Sailing Lore is master and Sea Myth Lore is expert in the export. His biography fields are blank.

The ancestry, background, and selected abilities below come from that file. The Daggerheart trait allocation, Experience wording, community, and level mapping are proposed conversion choices. They do not assert missing details about Kass's history or personality.

## Level-one starting build

| Choice | Selection |
| --- | --- |
| Class and subclass | Gunslinger, Drifter foundation |
| Domains | Bone and Artifice |
| Ancestry | Katari, using its normal features |
| Community | Seaborne, using its normal feature |
| Traits | Agility +2, Strength +0, Finesse +1, Instinct +1, Presence +0, Knowledge −1 |
| Experiences | Seasoned Pirate +2; Sure Footing in the Rigging +2 |
| Starting cards | Deft Maneuvers, Bone 1; Make Do, Artifice 1 |
| Primary weapon | Workshop Boarding Pistol, d6 physical, Far, one-handed |
| Secondary weapon | Core Shortsword, d8 physical, Melee, one-handed, Paired +2 |
| Armor | Core Leather Armor, Armor Score 3, base thresholds 6/13 |
| Evasion / HP / Stress | 10 / 6 / 6 |
| Proficiency | 1 |
| Final armor thresholds | 7/14 after adding level 1 |

The pistol's Adaptable feature lets Kass attack with Agility, matching the Shortsword. At Melee range, Blade and Powder adds +1 to the roll. A normal pistol hit there deals **d6+2**, including Paired; at longer ranges it deals **d6**. A Shortsword hit deals **d8**. Katari's Feline Instincts can affect eligible Agility rolls, using the normal ancestry rule and Hope cost.

Seaborne's existing community feature tracks its own tokens. That is separate from this package, which adds no firearm or class resource pool. If the table wants a different community, choose it for Kass's upbringing rather than to erase his sailing expertise; that expertise also lives in his Experience.

Knowledge −1 is a build allocation, not a claim that Kass is unintelligent. This version emphasizes movement, instinct, and hands-on improvisation. He can select Finesse-based Artifice abilities and use a relevant Experience on an appropriate roll. A more technically inclined version can trade some starting trait emphasis into Knowledge.

## Level-five established-adventurer example

Level 5 is a useful **playtest assumption**, not a mathematical conversion of PF2e level 11. It lets us test the Drifter specialization immediately. Use the party's chosen Daggerheart level for an actual campaign port.

### Advancement ledger

| Level reached | Two chosen advancements | Automatic new domain card | Tier achievement |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 2 | +1 Agility and Finesse, mark both; +1 HP slot | I See it Coming, Bone 1 | Proficiency becomes 2; add Sea Myths and Omens +2 |
| 3 | +1 Instinct and Presence, mark both; +1 Stress slot | Tactician, Bone 3 | None |
| 4 | +1 Evasion; +1 HP slot | Catch Line, Artifice 3 | None |
| 5 | Take Drifter specialization; +1 Agility and Finesse, mark both | Signature Move, Bone 5 | Clear old trait marks first; Proficiency becomes 3; add A Friend in Every Port +2 |

The two HP selections use the two tier-two HP advancements. No optional Proficiency advancement or extra domain-card advancement is taken. The build owns six domain cards at level 5 and can keep five in its loadout.

| Statistic | Level-five result |
| --- | --- |
| Traits | Agility +4, Strength +0, Finesse +3, Instinct +2, Presence +1, Knowledge −1 |
| Evasion | 11, before situational effects |
| HP / Stress slots | 8 / 7 |
| Proficiency / tier | 3 / 3 |
| Experiences | The four listed Experiences, each +2 |
| Subclass cards | Drifter foundation and specialization |

For an equipment-complete test at this tier, give him an **Advanced Workshop Boarding Pistol**, an **Advanced Shortsword**, and **Advanced Leather Armor**. These are GM-provided test equipment, not automatic level-up rewards. Their profiles come from the package and the existing tier-three equipment table.

- Pistol: **3d6+6**, Far, one-handed; **3d6+10** against a target in Melee while Paired +4 applies.
- Shortsword: **3d8+4**, Melee, one-handed. Paired modifies primary damage, not this secondary attack.
- Armor: Armor Score **5**; base thresholds **11/27**, final thresholds **16/32** after level 5.
- Blade and Powder adds **+1 to the attack roll**, not damage. With Agility, a qualifying attack begins at +5 before other modifiers or an Experience.
- Finish the Job deals **3d6** physical damage on its qualifying failure, once per rest for 1 Stress. It does not add +6 from the pistol, Paired +4, or any other damage bonus.

### Loadout

1. **Deft Maneuvers:** the boarding rush.
2. **I See it Coming:** reacting to a shot or thrown weapon.
3. **Tactician:** setting up the crew and coordinating attacks.
4. **Catch Line:** rigging and practical restraint with a line.
5. **Signature Move:** choose a specific repeatable sword-and-pistol maneuver using that card's normal rules.

**Vault:** Make Do. Swap it into the loadout through the normal rules when improvised tools are more useful. These are ordinary domain choices; no converted PF2e feat grants a bonus card.

## What carries across

| PF2e identity in the export | Daggerheart treatment |
| --- | --- |
| Catfolk, climbing and falling prowess | Katari; Agility; rigging Experience; Bone movement cards |
| Way of the Drifter; Sword and Pistol | Blade and Powder and two legal equipped weapons |
| Drifter's Juke; Running Reload | Keep Moving and Through the Fray; reloads remain descriptive |
| Finish the Job | The limited specialization feature; different resolution and damage scale |
| Fake Out; Broadside Buckos; Warrior's Rally | Expose, Help an Ally, Tactician, and crew-focused narration |
| Trick Shot; Bullet Split | Trick Shot; ordinary multi-target weapon or domain options where selected |
| Accidental Shot; Cat's Luck; Lucky Escape | Katari's existing reroll feature, I See it Coming, and the description of favorable rolls; no extra imported reroll pool |
| Pirate Dedication; Sailing and Sea Myth lore | Seaborne and specific Experiences |
| Wrist Grappler and practical equipment | Physical kit, Catch Line, and later Artifice choices |
| Sunken Pistol's personality | Its voice, appearance, and motivations can remain fiction |
| Sunken Pistol's supernatural powers | Requires a separate magic-item conversion; not silently folded into class balance |

Duplicate Sword and Pistol and Walk the Plank entries in the export are not additional Daggerheart features. The rune effect originating from another actor is not treated as Kass's permanent class ability. PF2e item runes, proficiency ranks, action costs, and 107 current HP are not copied numerically.

## A short scene to try

An enemy captain has a key on a chain, a pistol, and two sailors blocking the gangway. Kass wants the prisoner free and his crew aboard.

Start by deciding what matters most. A normal attack can remove a sailor through damage. Expose can create an opening against the captain. Manipulate might sever the exposed key-chain, using a GM-set Difficulty and a plausible landing position. A paid Against the Odds attack against the captain can hurt them and disarm their pistol, but cannot also break the key-chain as an extra effect.

Use the location to distinguish the subclasses during testing: a Drifter benefits from joining the crowded deck; a Sharpshooter benefits from climbing to a position with a clear angle. Neither receives free additional turns because its player describes several movements or shots.
