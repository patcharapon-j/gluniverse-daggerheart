# Flint

Domain · Draft 4 · 21 abilities

Flint is the domain of the steady hand and the long shot. Its practitioners pick their moment, find the weak point, and hold their nerve while everything around them comes apart. Flint is spark and stone at once: the instant of the shot, and the hardness that makes it possible.

Flint has two threads:

- **Aim:** what happens to the target. Drawing a bead, reaching farther, finding the weak point, and the one shot that changes the fight.
- **Nerve:** what happens to you. Flint pays you for staking something and for holding on when it goes wrong: Stress marked on purpose, rolls failed with Fear, and the moment everything is marked.

Flint never moves you and never dodges for you. That is Bone's.

Every Flint card is an ability. Cards that say "ranged weapon" are about shooting, but most of the deck works for any weapon and any class.

## Level 1

**Draw a Bead** · Level 1 · Recall 0
**Spend a Hope** to draw a bead on a target you can see within Far range. Until you choose to attack a different creature, the target is defeated, or it leaves your sight, your attacks against it gain a +1 bonus and you add a **d4** to your damage rolls against it. Attacks you make in response to an adversary's action don't end the bead.

**Under Fire** · Level 1 · Recall 1
When you take Major or greater damage, you can **mark a Stress**. On your next action roll, you can roll a **d20** as your Hope Die.

**Cold Read** · Level 1 · Recall 0
Once per rest, when you size someone up in a tense conversation, make an **Instinct Roll** against them. On a success, ask the GM one of the following questions, which they answer truthfully, and gain advantage on your next action roll against that person:
- What do they want right now?
- What are they afraid of?
- What are they hiding from me?

## Level 2

**Long Shot** · Level 2 · Recall 1
Increase the range of your ranged weapons with a range of Far to Very Far. When you succeed on an attack against a target at Very Far range, add a **d6** to your damage roll.

**Name the Stakes** · Level 2 · Recall 1
Once per rest, before you make an action roll, name what you stand to lose if it fails. Gain advantage on the roll. If the roll fails, **mark 2 Stress**.

## Level 3

**Covering Fire** · Level 3 · Recall 1
**Spend a Hope** and make an attack with a ranged weapon against a target within range. On a success, deal damage, and the target becomes temporarily *Suppressed*. While Suppressed, its attacks against anyone other than you are made with disadvantage.

**Grit** · Level 3 · Recall 1
When you fail a roll with Fear, place a token on this card, up to a maximum equal to your Proficiency. When you deal damage, you can spend any number of tokens from this card to add a **d6** to the damage roll for each token spent. When you take a long rest, clear all tokens from this card.

## Level 4

**Trick Shot** · Level 4 · Recall 1
**Spend a Hope** to make an attack with a ranged weapon against an object within range that hasn't been used this way this scene (Difficulty 12), such as a rope, a keg or a beam, and describe what the shot sets off. On a success, each adversary within Very Close range of the object must make a **Reaction Roll (15)**. Those who fail take your weapon's damage and are temporarily *Restrained*.

**Stare Down** · Level 4 · Recall 1
Once per rest, when you face down a creature in a tense standoff, make a **Presence Roll** against them. On a success, they must choose one: back down and not attack you for the rest of the scene unless you attack them first, or **mark 2 Stress** and make their next attack against you with disadvantage.

## Level 5

**Pick Your Spot** · Level 5 · Recall 1
When you succeed on an attack against a target that was *Vulnerable* or *Restrained* before you attacked, add a **d10** to your damage roll.

**All In** · Level 5 · Recall 2
Once per long rest, before you make an action roll, spend any number of Hope. Gain a +2 bonus to the roll for each Hope spent. If the roll fails, **mark a Stress** for each Hope you spent.

## Level 6

**Shoot It Out of Their Hand** · Level 6 · Recall 2
When you succeed on an attack with a ranged weapon, you can **spend a Hope** to name one of the target's Action features. They can't use it until the GM spends a Fear to restore it. A target can have only one feature disabled this way at a time.

**Nothing Left to Lose** · Level 6 · Recall 1
While all of your Stress slots are marked, your attack rolls have advantage.

## Level 7

**Flint-Touched** · Level 7 · Recall 1
When 4 or more of the domain cards in your loadout are from the Flint domain, gain the following benefits:
- +1 bonus to Finesse
- Once per rest, when you make an attack with a ranged weapon that rolls with Fear, you can **mark a Stress** to make it roll with Hope instead.

**Steady Hand** · Level 7 · Recall 1
Your attacks with ranged weapons can't be made with disadvantage. When an effect would give you disadvantage on one, gain a +1 bonus to the roll instead.

## Level 8

**Killshot** · Level 8 · Recall 2
When you succeed on an attack with a ranged weapon, you can **mark 2 Stress** to roll its damage with a +2 bonus to your Proficiency. The target's resistance doesn't apply, and it can't reduce this damage.

**Poker Face** · Level 8 · Recall 1
Once per session, when someone calls your bluff, you can double down. Make a **Presence Roll** with advantage against them. On a success, they believe you until they see clear proof otherwise. On a failure, **mark 2 Stress**.

## Level 9

**Through and Through** · Level 9 · Recall 2
Once per rest, when you succeed on an attack with a ranged weapon, you can **spend 2 Hope** to send the shot through the target. Use the same attack roll against up to two other targets within Very Close range of the first, and deal your full damage to each of them you succeed against.

**Parley** · Level 9 · Recall 2
Once per session, during a fight, call for a parley, **mark a Stress**, and make a **Presence Roll** against the leader of your enemies. On a success, adversaries who can hear you won't attack until you or an ally acts against them, or until the GM spends a Fear to end it.

## Level 10

**Deadeye** · Level 10 · Recall 2
Once per long rest, **spend 2 Hope** to make an attack with a ranged weapon, with advantage, against a target you can see, ignoring your weapon's range. On a success, treat it as a critical success, and the target must mark 2 additional Hit Points.

**Last Word** · Level 10 · Recall 3
When you mark your last Hit Point, before you make a death move, you can make an attack with a weapon against a target within your weapon's range. On a success, deal maximum damage, clear 2 Hit Points, and don't make a death move. On a failure, clear a Hit Point and don't make a death move. Either way, place this card in your vault.
