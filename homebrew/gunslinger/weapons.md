# Expanded firearms

Version 0.1 · Six primary families, with a secondary compact-pistol configuration

These are physical, nonmagical black-powder weapons for any class that can equip them. They add **24 primary profiles and four secondary profiles** across tiers 1–4. All use ordinary weapon attack and damage rules. There is no additional firearm proficiency requirement.

## Collection rules

Reloading, priming, and routine maintenance are part of the description of an attack. Do not count shots or roll for a reload. The repeating mechanisms on a pepperbox describe its feature; they do not grant extra attacks or imply an ammunition counter.

The weapons are loud, produce smoke, need suitable powder and shot, and require ordinary care. Their report can reveal a position or have other consequences in the fiction. The absence of ammunition counting does not produce ammunition when the character has none, make black powder waterproof, or make a shot silent. The collection introduces no automatic misfire rule.

Existing weapons retain their own names, damage, and features, including Reloading or Six Shot where printed. A character choosing this collection uses the complete profile below, not its damage combined with a feature from another firearm. A reskinned existing weapon keeps its complete existing profile instead.

Upgraded versions replace earlier equipment. They are not automatically granted on leveling. Follow the campaign's ordinary loot, crafting, purchase, and equipment-tier practices. The table establishes no gold prices.

### Primary weapon features

**Concealable.** Gain advantage on action rolls to conceal this weapon on your person. This does not grant advantage on an attack or make the shot quiet.

**Adaptable.** You can use Agility instead of Finesse for attacks with this weapon.

**Long Stock.** Attacks with this weapon against a target within Melee range have disadvantage.

**Spread.** Before attacking, you can mark 1 Stress to also target a second adversary within Melee range of the first and within this weapon's range. Apply the same attack roll to both targets and roll damage once for those hit.

**Follow Through.** After rolling this weapon's damage, you can mark 1 Stress to reroll one of its damage dice, keeping the new result. Use this feature at most once per attack.

**Recoil.** While this weapon is equipped, take a −1 penalty to Evasion.

These are each the weapon's single feature. Spread is optional: declining it allows a single-target Trick Shot. The shot's fiction still uses a focused discharge. When using Trick Shot or Against the Odds, Spread and all other added-target features are unavailable. A damage-replacing Trick Shot has no damage roll for Follow Through to modify. Against the Odds does roll damage and permits Follow Through, Paired, and other damage modifiers under its stated restrictions.

## Secondary configuration

The Sleeve Flintlock is the Pocket Flintlock family configured for use beside a primary melee weapon. Equip it as a secondary weapon using the ordinary rules. It does not add a hand, weapon slot, or extra attack. It can also make its own attacks using its printed profile where the normal secondary-weapon rules permit.

| Configuration | Trait | Maximum range | Burden | Tier 1 | Tier 2 | Tier 3 | Tier 4 | Feature |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Sleeve Flintlock | Finesse | Very Close | One-handed | d6 | d6+2 | d6+4 | d6+6 | Paired |

**Paired.** Add a bonus to primary weapon damage against targets within Melee range: **+2 at tier 1, +3 at tier 2, +4 at tier 3, or +5 at tier 4**. Add the bonus once to a damaging attack. It does not apply when Trick Shot replaces that damage.

The Sleeve Flintlock uses the same Workshop tier prefixes. Its feature replaces Concealable; it does not have both. A Drifter can also use a primary Boarding Pistol with an existing Shortsword or Small Dagger secondary.

## Choosing a weapon

| Weapon | Reason to choose it | What you give up |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Pocket Flintlock | A compact weapon and a larger die at short range | Useful range beyond Very Close |
| Boarding Pistol | A free hand and choice of Agility or Finesse | One flat damage compared with the core one-handed crossbow at tier 1 |
| Long Musket | Reach and Agility with a useful damage die | Both hands and accuracy at Melee range |
| Deck Blunderbuss | A resource-funded shot against two nearby adversaries | Reach, a hand, and substantial single-target damage |
| Turning Pepperbox | A one-handed weapon with optional damage correction | Far range and Stress when using its feature |
| Brace Cannon | Strength-based ranged damage with a larger die | Both hands and 1 Evasion |
| Sleeve Flintlock | Sword-first Drifter play with a secondary firearm | Lower damage die and range than many primary firearms |

The collection intentionally has no general bonus for being a gun. Armor works normally against bullets. None of these weapons has armor penetration, direct damage, extra Proficiency, or an increased critical range.

## Tier arithmetic

Primary flat bonuses increase by 3 per tier, matching a common progression in the local equipment tables. Secondary flat bonuses increase by 2; the Paired bonus increases by 1. Higher tiers do not increase range or reduce burden. These are starting balance assumptions, not a conversion of PF2e striking runes.

For a simple comparison at tier 1 and Proficiency 1, before accuracy, secondary features, or resource spending: the Boarding Pistol averages **3.5**, the Pocket Flintlock **4.5**, the Long Musket **5.5**, the Deck Blunderbuss and Turning Pepperbox **4.5**, and the Brace Cannon **6.5** damage. At Proficiency 2, the Boarding Pistol averages **7**, not 3.5 twice plus its flat bonus twice. Thresholds and targets hit matter more than these isolated averages.

## Kass's Sunken Pistol

For the initial playtest, a Workshop Boarding Pistol can carry the Sunken Pistol's barnacles, voice, and desire for treasure as its fiction. Its special underwater fire, supernatural damage, independent intimidation, and water-breathing powers are not included in this nonmagical weapon profile. A full magical Sunken Pistol is a separate future item conversion; its powers should be budgeted and tested independently of Gunslinger.
