# Corsair

Gunslinger subclass · Draft 4

Play the Corsair if you want to fight with a blade in one hand and a pistol in the other, and stand between your crew and anyone who would hurt them.

Spellcast trait: none.

## Foundation features

**Steel and Shot:** While you have a Melee weapon and a ranged weapon active, when you succeed on an attack with one of them, your next attack with the other against the same target gains a +2 bonus.

**Unshackled:** You have advantage on rolls to escape bindings or restraints. Once per rest, you can clear the *Restrained* condition from yourself or an ally within Very Close range.

## Specialization features

**Answer for It:** Once per GM turn, when an adversary within range of your active ranged weapon succeeds on an attack against one or more allies, you can **mark a Stress** to make an attack against them with that weapon. On a success, they must mark a Hit Point. This attack generates Hope or Fear as normal. Afterward, the GM's turn continues.

**All Hands:** Once per long rest, when you and your allies face a danger together, call out your orders. You and each ally who can hear you can clear 2 Stress or gain a Hope.

## Mastery features

**Two Birds:** When you call your shot, you can choose two different options instead of one. The Difficulty still increases by only 2, and you pay the cost of each option you choose.

**Infamy:** Once per long rest, when you reveal yourself to your enemies, describe the story they've heard about you and make a **Presence Roll (15)**. On a success, the GM loses a Fear and each adversary who can hear you must mark a Stress.

## Kass

Kass, captain of the Argo and once "Odd Eye Kass," is the reference character for this subclass. The cards carry Kass's archetype and none of Kass's named things.

| Kass in Tide of Destiny | Where it lands |
| --- | --- |
| Pistol and unnamed sword (PF2e Way of the Drifter) | Steel and Shot |
| Born a slave in the Argo's hold; collar and ear tag | Unshackled |
| The Rules of the Argo: no one aboard is harmed; "I want them to feel everything they did to us" | Answer for It |
| The captain who calls the vote and holds the crew together | All Hands |
| The trick shots of S03E15: the barrel, the bridge joints, the neck | Two Birds; Flint's *Trick Shot* |
| Odd Eye's reputation | Infamy |
| Reading people by small details | Flint's *Cold Read* (level 1) |

Victoria (the orichalcum pistol) and the Sunken Pistol stay as campaign loot, not subclass features.

**Suggested Kass build at level 1:** Katari ancestry. Revolver primary. Small Dagger secondary (Finesse, Melee, d8, Paired), described as a short boarding cutlass. Flint cards *Cold Read* and *Draw a Bead*.
