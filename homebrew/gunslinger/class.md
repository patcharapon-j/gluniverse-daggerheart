# Gunslinger

Version 0.1 · Playtest rules

Gunslingers use nerve, precision, and an eye for opportunity to change a dangerous situation with a shot.

| Starting statistic | Value |
| --- | --- |
| Domains | Bone and Artifice |
| Evasion | 10 |
| Hit Points | 6 |
| Stress slots | 6, as normal |
| Spellcast trait | None |

Choose Drifter or Sharpshooter. Use the normal starting trait array, Experiences, equipment allowances, and advancement rules. A suggested array is in the Kass example; the class does not lock weapon attacks to one trait.

## Class feature: Trick Shot

Before making a **single-target firearm attack**, declare one effect from the table below and describe the shot that would produce it. The target must be visible, within the firearm's range, and meet the effect's requirements. The GM establishes whether the shot is possible before you roll.

On a success, you can **spend 1 Hope** to apply the declared effect **instead of all damage from that attack**. Otherwise, resolve the attack normally. A successful roll with Hope can supply the Hope you spend. You cannot change the declared effect after rolling.

| Effect | Result |
| --- | --- |
| **Expose** | The adversary becomes temporarily **Vulnerable**. Describe how the shot makes them expose themselves or lose their footing. |
| **Disarm** | One object the adversary is holding and could drop lands on an unoccupied surface within Melee range of them. They can recover it through the normal fiction and GM moves. This neither removes their other attacks nor guarantees they must spend an entire spotlight retrieving it. |
| **Herd** | Move an adversary capable of moving up to a Melee distance to an unoccupied position you can see within the firearm's range. The route and destination must be safe for that adversary. The shot provokes movement; it cannot drag, lift, or launch a target. |
| **Pin** | Fasten an adversary's loose clothing or carried material to a nearby fixed surface, making them temporarily **Restrained**. There must be both a suitable material and an anchor within their Melee range. |
| **Manipulate** | Strike an exposed, nonmagical object to sever a thin cord, break a fragile component, or move a small unsecured object up to a Melee distance along a supporting surface. The GM sets the object's Difficulty before the roll. This cannot operate a complex device or destroy a substantial barrier in one shot. |

When you replace damage, remove bonus damage and other offensive weapon or on-hit effects as well. The roll still counts as a successful attack for your own movement and resource features. It cannot be combined with additional attacks, additional targets, or a Tag Team Roll. An ordinary critical success still grants its normal Hope and Stress recovery, but a damage-replacing Trick Shot deals no critical damage.

## Hope feature: Against the Odds

Before attempting a Trick Shot, **spend 3 Hope**. On a success, deal the firearm attack's normal damage to its target and apply the declared trick-shot effect. You do not pay the ordinary 1 Hope cost. On a failure, the Hope remains spent and neither benefit occurs.

The attack remains single-target. Damage dice, flat damage bonuses such as Paired, critical damage, and damage rerolls apply normally. Do not apply additional conditions, movement of the target, forced HP or Stress marking, or other target-affecting riders beyond the declared Trick Shot effect and the HP loss caused by this damage. Your own movement and resource features, including Keep Moving, remain available. A Manipulate shot damages only the object it targets; it does not also damage a nearby adversary. This feature grants no extra attack.

## Drifter

Choose Drifter to move through close combat with a blade in one hand and a pistol in the other.

### Foundation card

**Blade and Powder.** While wielding a one-handed firearm and a one-handed melee weapon, gain **+1 to single-target attack rolls** made with either against an adversary within Melee range when you describe using both in the sequence. Choose one weapon for the roll's trait and damage; do not combine damage profiles.

**Keep Moving.** After you succeed on such an attack, you can move without a roll to another unoccupied position within Melee range of that target along a clear route.

### Specialization card

**Through the Fray.** When you use Keep Moving, you can **spend 1 Hope** to move anywhere within Very Close range of your starting position instead, along a clear route.

**Finish the Job.** Once per rest, when a Blade and Powder attack fails, you can **mark 1 Stress** to strike with the other weapon in the sequence. Deal physical damage to the same target equal to **a number of d6s equal to your tier**. This is a fixed damage roll, not another attack; add no other damage bonuses or on-hit effects. Resolve the original failure and any GM move normally.

### Mastery card

**Close Quarters Legend.** While wielding the weapons required by Blade and Powder, gain **+1 Evasion** against attacks made by adversaries within Melee range.

**Nothing Wasted.** Once per long rest, after a successful Blade and Powder firearm attack, you can apply one eligible Trick Shot effect to that target while retaining the attack's normal damage. You do not spend Hope. Declare the effect at this point, but all other Against the Odds restrictions apply. Do not use this feature on an attack for which you already paid for Against the Odds.

## Sharpshooter

Choose Sharpshooter to find useful firing positions and make precision count across the battlefield.

### Foundation card

**Find the Angle.** When making a single-target firearm attack against a visible target beyond Melee range, you can ignore disadvantage caused by that target's partial cover if your position offers a plausible exposed angle. Describe the angle. This does not reveal an unseen target or bypass a solid obstruction.

**Sure Footing.** Gain advantage on action rolls made to climb, balance, or cross precarious ground specifically to reach a firing position you can identify. This does not grant extra movement or automatically succeed.

### Specialization card

**Measured Shot.** When you succeed on a single-target firearm attack against a target beyond Close range and roll its damage, you can **mark 1 Stress** to reroll one damage die. Use the new result. You can do this only once for that attack.

**Thread the Needle.** You can use Manipulate to operate one exposed simple lever, latch, or switch with a successful shot, provided moving it requires neither sustained pressure nor a sequence of operations.

### Mastery card

**One Shot, Two Problems.** Once per long rest, when Against the Odds succeeds against an adversary beyond Melee range, you can apply a second, different eligible Trick Shot effect to that same target. Describe how one shot causes both effects. Resolve damage only once; this grants no extra target or attack.

**Hold Your Nerve.** When Measured Shot rerolls a die, you can keep either result.

## Character prompts

### Starting possessions

Choose a battered tool inherited from your teacher or an unfired bullet engraved with a name. These possessions provide story prompts, not mechanical bonuses.

### Look

Possible clothing: a patched sea coat, a formal shooting jacket, a work apron, a dust-stained cloak, or an embroidered sash.

Possible bearing: a cardsharp, a watchmaker, a host, a stray cat, or a weary teacher. Interpret these freely.

### Background questions

1. Who taught you that an accurate shot and a wise shot are different things? When did you ignore them?
2. What did you once break with a single shot that you still hope to repair?
3. Who recognizes your weapon, and what do they believe you owe them?

### Connection questions

1. What have you trusted me to repair that matters more to you than its price?
2. When did one of my clever plans put you in danger, and what have I promised since?
3. What signal do we use when you need me to create an opening?

## Adjudication and examples

### Targeting the situation

An Expose shot can kick up splinters or force a guard to raise their arms. It does not require the target to understand a threat. Herd requires a target able to respond by moving; it is not telekinesis. Pin requires an actual connection to an anchor. An unclothed stone creature cannot be pinned by inventing a coat after the roll.

Disarm uses the adversary's Difficulty, not a separate easy object Difficulty. Against the Odds can therefore deal damage to that adversary while disarming them. Manipulate instead targets the object and its GM-assigned Difficulty. An unattended rope and a rope actively protected by a guard need not have the same Difficulty.

Disarm cannot remove claws, teeth, internal mechanisms, or spellcasting. A staff falling to the floor does not erase every magical feature in the adversary's stat block. Recovery should follow the scene: an object at their feet may be recovered as part of a move; an object now contested by a PC creates a different problem.

Herd's safe-route requirement means the feature cannot send someone off a cliff, into fire, or into a known harmful trap. A player can still attempt a different environmental stunt under the normal rules. Manipulate can likewise have consequences in the fiction, but an explosive barrel or collapsing building uses GM-adjudicated environmental effects, not an automatic grant of weapon damage to everyone nearby.

### Applying conditions

Expose and Pin follow ordinary temporary-condition rules. They are not stackable accuracy penalties or stun effects. A restrained archer can still shoot. An adversary can normally spend its spotlight clearing a temporary condition; a GM may also use applicable normal ways to end temporary effects. Do not attach an arbitrary number of rounds.

### Sword and pistol example

Kass has a Boarding Pistol as his primary weapon and a Shortsword as his secondary. An enemy is in Melee range. He describes slashing to draw their guard, then firing beneath it. He uses the pistol's Finesse and damage profile and adds Blade and Powder's +1 to the attack roll. A normal damaging hit can benefit from the Shortsword's existing Paired feature where applicable. It does not add the Shortsword's damage dice.

If Kass declared Expose and spends 1 Hope after succeeding, he deals no damage, including no Paired bonus. He makes the enemy temporarily Vulnerable and can use Keep Moving. If he paid 3 Hope for Against the Odds before rolling, he instead deals damage and exposes that one target.

### Failure example

At specialization, Kass misses that close-range shot. He marks 1 Stress and consumes his once-per-rest Finish the Job use to deal tier d6 physical damage with his sword. This does not change a failure into a success, generate Hope, prevent Fear, or delay the GM's response beyond resolving the feature.

### Multiclassing and equipment

Use normal multiclass acquisition rules. Neither subclass supplies a Spellcast trait for spells acquired elsewhere. Bone and Artifice abilities need none. Gunslinger does not ignore weapon burden or grant another primary weapon slot. Existing firearms you can legally equip can use Gunslinger features, but retain their printed features and reload rules; the collection in this package uses narrative reloading instead. Normal restrictions on magic-damage weapons still apply.

Trick Shot's additional-target restriction matters even if another class, domain, or item would normally add targets. Finish the Job is limited to a failed single-target attack meeting Blade and Powder's conditions. It cannot trigger from another damage roll or from its own damage.
