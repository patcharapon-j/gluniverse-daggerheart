# Gunslinger

Draft 4 · shipped in `src/packs-src/gluniverse-gunslinger.mjs`

Gunslingers are duelists of the long shot and the steady hand, who win fights by calling where the bullet goes before it leaves the barrel. Some learned on the decks of raiding ships, some on frontier roads with a price on their heads, and some in the drilled ranks of an army they later walked away from. What they share is nerve: the willingness to take the harder shot because the easier one doesn't end the fight. A gunslinger's reputation tends to arrive a little before they do, and they rarely do much to correct it.

## Gunslinger's Hope feature

**Quickdraw:** When an adversary within range of your active ranged weapon makes an attack against you or an ally, you can **spend 3 Hope** to make an attack against them with that weapon before their attack roll is made. Their attack is made with disadvantage. On a success, deal damage, and if this defeats them, their attack doesn't happen. If their attack has several targets, it's made with disadvantage against each of them. This attack can be a called shot and generates Hope or Fear as normal. Afterward, the GM's turn continues.

## Class feature

**Call Your Shot:** Before you make an attack with a ranged weapon, you can call your shot against one target. Choose one of the following options and describe the shot that would make it happen. The attack's Difficulty increases by 2. On a success, deal damage with a +1 bonus to your Proficiency and apply the option you chose.

- **Rattle:** The target must mark a Stress.
- **Disarm:** The target's next attack is made with disadvantage.
- **Expose:** **Mark a Stress** to make the target temporarily *Vulnerable*.
- **Pin:** **Mark a Stress** to make the target temporarily *Restrained*. A flying target that becomes Restrained lands.

When a called shot succeeds, you don't roll for your weapon's Reloading feature, and if you spent an Ammo token to make the attack, regain it. If the attack has more than one target, the option and the Proficiency bonus apply only to the target you called.

| Armor | Any tier 1 armor; Gambeson Armor is suggested |

The Revolver, Small Revolver and Lasso are from SRD 2.0's Western Campaigns (p. 197), and they are this class's default arsenal at every tier. The printed firearms (Blunderbuss, Black Powder Revolver, Hand Cannon and the rest) also work with every Gunslinger feature, because nothing in this class names a specific weapon.

**Suggested traits:** Agility +1, Strength −1, Finesse +2, Instinct +1, Presence 0, Knowledge 0

## Background questions

Answer any of the following background questions. You can also create your own questions.

- Who taught you to shoot, and what did they make you promise before they put a gun in your hand?
- What did you lose the one time you hesitated?
- Someone is still looking for you over a shot you fired. Who are they, and were you in the right?

## Connections

Ask your fellow players one of the following questions for their character to answer, or create your own questions.

- Why do I trust you to stand where I'm aiming?
- What did you see me do that you still haven't forgiven?
- What tune do I whistle while I clean my guns, and do you know where I learned it?
