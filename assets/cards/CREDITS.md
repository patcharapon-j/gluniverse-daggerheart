# Card art credits

The images in this folder are the official Daggerheart card header art,
fetched from the Daggerheart Card Creator by `tools/fetch-cards.mjs`.
They are the property of Darrington Press and are **not** covered by the
Darrington Press Community Gaming License, which grants the rules text
only. They are included here for local play; do not redistribute them.

Regenerate this file with `node tools/fetch-cards.mjs`.
