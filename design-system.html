Typography

Hierarchy comes from scale and role, not extreme weight. Keep all interface text readable at play distance.

Lyra Vey
Deft Deceiver

Google Sans leads the display system: confident enough for technical surfaces, human enough for fantasy and character-focused play.

Midnight / Utility / Active · Level 01 · Recall 0

Type roles

  • Display: Google Sans, weights 500–700.
  • Body: Avenir Next or Gill Sans, 10–16px depending on surface.
  • Metadata: monospace, uppercase, wide tracking.
  • Never use more than two visible type families at once.
  • Numbers are large, calm, and optically centered.

Usage rules

These constraints keep new surfaces recognizable and playable across many Daggerheart settings.

Rules before spectacle

Numbers, costs, traits, and consequences remain readable before material effects are considered.

Gold must mean something

Reserve it for Hope, advancement, earned status, and active cost—not generic decoration.

One tactile hero

Let one object or panel carry the strongest material treatment. Supporting surfaces stay quieter.

Cards remain objects

Domain, ancestry, community, weapon, armor, and equipment cards share collectible anatomy.

Texture is selective

Use fine grain and foil only where they create hierarchy or material distinction.

Setting neutral by default

Avoid genre-specific machinery, parchment, or ornament unless a campaign theme explicitly supplies it.
