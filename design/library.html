The content library

One place where the GM decides what this world plays with. A source is the unit, and it is the unit a table decides in: "core and Hope and Fear, with our own homebrew". It is not the pack — the domain deck holds the corebook's nine, Dread, and Root and Void in one list — so every document carries its source and the pack is only the unit for somebody else's homebrew.
