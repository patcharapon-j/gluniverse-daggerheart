// THE GAPThe engine has been ready for this since it was written

rollDuality already takes advantage as a signed count of d6, mods as labelled terms and dc as a target number. rollTrait and rollAttack take experiences on top of that. Nothing in the sheet passed any of it. So this was never a feature being added to the roll engine — it is the one surface that had never existed, and the engine is its specification.

There was a second gap, and it was worse. actions.ts stamps every Experience term spent: true, which the chat plate draws in gold — the currency that paid for it. Nothing ever called spendHope. An Experience was free and the card said it was not.

// THE CONSTRAINTThe empty state is the common state

Opening on the click that used to roll puts this in front of the most frequent gesture in the game — including every roll that needs nothing from it. A level-1 character has no Experiences, nothing granting advantage and no reason to type a modifier, and they will meet this popover twice a minute for an hour. So the bare case is not an edge case being dutifully covered. It is the case that decided this. Roll-ready on the frame it opens, Enter before you have touched anything, Escape costing nothing, and visibly shorter when there is less to say.

// THE COMPONENTThe console, live

The real thing, not a specimen — it opens on <body>, anchors to what you pressed, and resolves to the arguments the engine accepts. Press a button and it opens beside it. Enter rolls, Shift+Enter reacts, Escape cancels, A/D/H work the advantage row and the digits tick Experiences. The result is printed underneath.
