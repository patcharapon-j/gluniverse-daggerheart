// A · THE WORST CASEBook of Ava, three ways

The plate is rigid — flex:0 0, so nothing about the text can move it. It is 50% unless the panel comes up short, and then it gives ground down to 40% and no further; below that the composition stops being a card with art on it. Only when 40% is still not enough does the type scale start. A is what ships — the band is what makes a grimoire skimmable rather than three paragraphs to read, and the plate concession is what pays for it. B and C stay here as the record of what the cheap form would have bought.

// B · THE SCALE IS EARNED, NOT GUESSEDSame card, four lengths

A layout that only works for the text I happened to test with is not a layout — authors will write long features. So the panel measures itself: .cnt is the one box allowed to clip, and the card gives ground in a fixed order — plate first, then prose. Wrapped text falls off with roughly the square of the scale — a smaller size gives both shorter lines and fewer of them — so a 12% cut buys ~23% of height. Chrome never scales: the corners, seam, badge, footer and tier bar are on raw cqw, so a compacted card still sits in a row without reading as a different component. Body leading is now 1.38 rather than 1.46 — these are two-to-four line runs in a fixed box, not body copy, and the tighter setting reads the same while giving back most of a line every four.
