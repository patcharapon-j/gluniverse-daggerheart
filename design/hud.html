// THE PLAYERPlay without the sheet

One rail, 96px, on the bottom edge. Two things stand on it: the character, rising out of the plinth, and the hand of cards. Everything else is a readout or a press inside the rail's height: Hope and the tracks, the traits over both weapons, and the class's features as rows of rules, the Hope feature first. Hope lights a halo behind the figure — gain one and the character warms before you have counted the gems. Synthetic sample data throughout; the portrait is the repo's art sample.

// THE GMAdversaries, and the whole party in one row

Same rail, same rules. The plinth is the selected adversary; the panel leads with its Difficulty in the family's diamond, then the tracks the GM marks, then every move as a chip — the one that spends Fear wears violet and nothing else does. The adversaries in play are a hand of compact cards, the same object as a player's loadout, each with its Hit Points along the top edge; the one on the plinth is lifted. The party stands where it does on a player's rail, above the plinth, as ringed seats.
