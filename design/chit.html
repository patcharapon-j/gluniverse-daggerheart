// THE OBJECTA counter you put down, not a box you cross off

Every other row of boxes on this sheet is printed and crossed off — Hit Points, Stress, Armor Slots, charges. The boxes are always there and only the marking varies. That premise is false for these: six of the pools have no maximum at all, several have one that moves when your Spellcast trait does, and one is place 1d4+1. So a chit exists or it does not, and an empty pool draws nothing.

the chit chamfered, four times

The family mark, stated four times. A corner cut off rather than turned — the same claim the sheet's bottom-right makes, applied all the way round. It is also what an actual cardboard counter looks like. The rhombus survives as the pip, which is the mark doing its ordinary job.

the disc the alternative

Foreign on purpose. A chit is the one object here that is not printed on the card, so being unlike the family may be right rather than a lapse. Against it: “nothing in this system has a rounded corner” is a rule that has never been broken.

// THE STATESCapped draws sockets; open does not

This is the schema showing through the surface honestly. A pool authored fixed:5 or trait:spellcast has a capacity worth seeing, so the remainder is drawn as empty sockets. An open pool — 1d4+1, or “equal to the Hit Points you marked” — has no capacity to draw, so the row ends at its last chit. At five held counters the row becomes one counter and a multiplier.

// THE MOTIONA drop and a lift, and nothing idle

Placing is a drop: the chit falls onto the card, lands slightly large and settles, with its shadow spreading and tightening behind it. Taking is a lift — it comes up toward you and away, because you picked it up; shrinking it into the card would be the card swallowing it. Refusal is the row flinching, never a dialog. There is no idle animation, for gem.css's reason: twelve things breathing on a sheet is twelve things asking for attention all day for nothing.

Only the difference moves. The row is rendered once and driven by setChits afterwards — the contract Gems and Marks already keep. Rebuilding the markup would cut a settle off mid-drop and restart it.
