// THE STONEThe dice are the gems, not d12 icons

Shared by all five, and it is the part that matters most. The rules are not being poetic — the Hope Die is Hope and the Fear Die is Fear — and this sheet already renders those two as edge-lit glass diamonds. So the duality dice here are those stones, carrying a numeral: flat saturated face, a brighter rim where the light leaves the edge, a bloom on a wrapper so the filter is not clipped away by the shape it describes. The losing die is not greyed out — it is unlit. Same stone, no light in it. An advantage d6 is neither Hope nor Fear, so it is neither stone: a notched chip carrying its own sign.
