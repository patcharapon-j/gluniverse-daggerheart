// DECIDEDB, with drag, and two arrivals

The vault tab holds both lists. Loadout on top, vault below, and every swap happens there. Click a vault row to arm it and a loadout row to land it; or drag one onto the other. Both paths run the same three lines and produce the same animation, so there is nothing to learn twice and nothing that can drift apart. Click-to-arm is the one that always works — touch, keyboard, a hand that cannot hold a button down — and drag is the one anyone who has used a deckbuilder tries first. Neither is the real one.

The motion is a FLIP, and it had to be. Committing a swap repaints the pane, which destroys every row — there is nothing left to transition. So the rects are measured before the repaint and again after, and the difference is played backwards: the row appears to travel from where it was to where it now is, having never moved. Every other row flips too, at a shorter duration, so the list closes over the gap instead of snapping. On a drop the card is already under the pointer, so its live rect overrides its captured one — it flies from the hand while everything else flies from where it was.

Two arrivals, same construction, opposite readings. A recall is the sheet committing: a lit band wipes left to right with a hard gold leading edge, corner brackets snap onto the two square corners, and the card's colour comes back up as it comes to hand. A shelve is that wipe run backwards and dark, with no accent and no brackets, and the folder tab slides in — filing something away should not be celebrated. Both are clipped to the row's own chamfer, so the light stops where the corner is cut; that one detail is most of why it reads as a machined panel rather than a glow.

Three bugs worth keeping. The drag was bound to .win, and picking a card up arms it, and arming repaints — so the drag spent the rest of its life talking to a detached tree: proxy followed the pointer, hit test found nothing, drop silently did nothing. It is bound to the layer now and looks the window up per call. The empty slot used height:100% and collapsed to 15px whenever it was alone in its grid row — the row it was 100% of is sized by its contents, and its only content was the slot; it takes the spine's own aspect-ratio:100/19 instead. And the shelve button was being swallowed by the swap's own "a control is a control" guard, which exists to keep other people's buttons out, not its own.

Rests are run at the table, for this version. No downtime moves, no short/long, nothing cleared automatically — the Resting switch is the whole of it, set by hand. That makes it the only thing on screen that knows whether a swap is free, so it says which state it is in rather than restating the rule: “free while resting” is true in both positions and answers nothing. While it is on the strip carries a gold edge, because nothing but a hand turns it off and the failure mode is a sheet quietly giving away Stress it should have charged.

// WHAT THE RULES SAYTwo prices, and the sheet already prints one of them

Five active domain cards, maximum. Subclass, ancestry and community don’t count toward it and are always active — which is why they live in the main tab and not here. Everything else you own sits in the vault, and there are exactly two ways to move a card out of it:

At the start of a rest, free, and before any downtime moves. Swap as many as you like. This is where nearly every swap happens.
Any other time, mark Stress equal to that card’s Recall Cost. That is the number already sitting in the corner chip of every domain row on this sheet — which means the price is already drawn, on the thing it prices, and has been since the first card template. The interaction just has to spend it.

One more, from the fine print: a spell already in play stays in play after you vault the card that cast it. So a vaulted card is not an undone card, and the vault tab should never read as a graveyard.

// THE PROBLEMA swap is two-sided, and the two sides are on different tabs

At 5/5 there is no such thing as “add a card”. Something leaves. So the gesture needs two picks, and the pick that decides the whole thing — is this better than the worst one I’m holding? — requires seeing both lists at once. Right now the loadout is in the main tab and the vault is in the vault tab, and you can’t see both. That is the actual thing to fix; the button is downstream of it.

// TRY ITOption B, live

Click a vault row to arm it, then click a loadout row to trade — or an empty slot to fill it. Escape or a click anywhere else cancels. The Stress is real: flip Resting off and the swap charges the incoming card’s Recall Cost against the track on the right, and refuses when you can’t pay. Hover a loadout row for shelve, which is free in both directions of the rules — only the recall is priced.
